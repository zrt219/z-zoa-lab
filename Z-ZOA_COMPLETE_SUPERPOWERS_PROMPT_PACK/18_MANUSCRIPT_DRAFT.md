**Z-ZOA: Out-of-Sample Reconstruction and H I Validation of Large-Scale Structure Across the Galactic Zone of Avoidance**

1. Abstract — written last from verified results.

8. Robustness/limitations.
9. Discussion in relation to prior ZoA/CF4 work.
10. Conclusions.
11. Data/code availability.
